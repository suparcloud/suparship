# suparShip Commercial Licensing

suparShip is open source software licensed under **AGPL v3**.

For many users, the AGPL license is perfectly suitable.

However, some use cases require a **commercial license**.

---

## When you NEED a commercial license

You need a commercial license if you:

- Offer suparShip as a **hosted or managed service**
- Provide suparShip to customers as part of a **SaaS or PaaS offering**
- Embed suparShip into a **closed-source or proprietary product**
- Do not want to share modifications to suparShip under AGPL
- Require legal assurances, SLAs, or enterprise support

---

## When you do NOT need a commercial license

You do **NOT** need a commercial license if you:

- Self-host suparShip internally
- Use suparShip to deploy proprietary or closed-source applications
- Modify suparShip for internal use only
- Evaluate or experiment with suparShip
- Use suparShip in demo, development, or staging environments

---

## What the commercial license provides

A commercial license typically includes:

- Permission to use suparShip without AGPL obligations
- Rights to offer suparShip as a service
- Optional enterprise support
- Optional managed installation & upgrades
- Optional hosted control plane (when available)

Exact terms depend on your use case.
